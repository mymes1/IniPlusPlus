# TIM RULES

*The Complete Story — Chapters 1–75*

## Chapter 1: The Worst Idea Ever

Monday
Okay, so today was supposed to be normal.
It wasn't.
It all started when I walked into school and saw a HUGE sign taped to the front doors.
STUDENT COUNCIL ELECTIONS — VOTE FOR YOUR LEADER!
I didn't really care about that stuff.
Until I saw who was running.
There was:
BRAD — the guy who thinks he's the strongest person alive.
JOSH — the guy who thinks he's the funniest person alive.
MASON — the guy who somehow gets free snacks from everyone.
And then there was...
ME.
Except I wasn't actually running.
Someone had written my name on the list.
I stared at it for about ten seconds.
"Who did this?" I asked.
My best friend, Noah, looked at the list.
Then he looked at me.
Then he started laughing.
"You should totally run."
"No."
"Why not?"
"Because I don't want to spend my free time doing school stuff."
Noah nodded.
"Fair."
Then he paused.
"But imagine if you won."
I thought about it.
Being the student council leader sounded pretty cool.
People would have to listen to me.
I could make rules.
I could change things.
I could—
"NO."
I looked over.
Brad was standing behind us.
"You're not becoming student council leader."
I raised an eyebrow.
"I wasn't planning to."
"Good."
Then Brad walked away like he had just won an argument.
Noah looked at me.
I looked at Noah.
He smiled.
I sighed.
"Fine."
"What?"
"I'm running."
And that's how I made the worst decision of my life.

LUNCH
By lunchtime, everyone somehow knew I was running.
I didn't even know how.
One kid came up to me and said,
"Tim! I'll vote for you if you make Fridays pizza day!"
Another said,
"Can you make homework illegal?"
Someone else asked,
"Can you make the teachers give us longer breaks?"
I started thinking this might actually be easy.
Then I realized something.
I didn't have a campaign.
Brad had posters.
Josh had buttons.
Mason was handing out sweets.
I had...
Nothing.
Noah looked at me.
"You need a slogan."
"A slogan?"
"Yeah."
I thought for a moment.
Then I said:
"TIM RULES."
Noah stared at me.
I stared at Noah.
"That's actually pretty good."
"Obviously."
By the end of lunch, we'd made three posters.
They weren't very good.
One of them said:
TIM RULES!
Another said:
VOTE TIM OR DON'T.
And the last one just said:
TIM
I thought the last one looked the most professional.

AFTER SCHOOL
When I got home, Mom asked me how school was.
"Fine."
"Anything interesting happen?"
I stopped.
I probably should have told her about the election.
But then I remembered something important.
Mom LOVES school stuff.
If I told her I was running for student council, she'd probably make me wear a shirt that said:
VOTE FOR TIM!
So I said:
"Nope."
I went upstairs.
I sat on my bed.
Then I looked at my campaign poster.
For some reason, I actually wanted to win.
Maybe being student council leader wouldn't be so bad.
Maybe I could actually change things.
Maybe people would finally—
BANG!
My bedroom door flew open.
Noah was standing there.
I jumped.
"HOW DID YOU GET IN HERE?!"
"Your mom let me in."
"What?"
He held up a piece of paper.
"The election is tomorrow."
I nearly fell off my bed.
"WHAT?!"
He smiled.
"Good luck, Mr. President."
Then he walked out.
I looked at my poster.
TIM RULES.
Yeah.
This was going to be a disaster.

## Chapter 2: The Campaign War

Tuesday
Today was my first official campaign day.
Which sounds important.
It wasn't.
It started with me walking into school and seeing Brad standing in the hallway surrounded by about twenty people.
He had a giant banner behind him that said:
VOTE BRAD!
Underneath it was a picture of Brad flexing.
I don't know who took the picture, but they probably need glasses.
Noah walked up beside me.
"Uh-oh."
"What?"
"Look."
I looked at the other side of the hallway.
There was my poster.
Except someone had drawn a mustache on my face.
I immediately knew who did it.
"BRAD!"
Brad turned around.
He smiled.
"What?"
"You drew on my poster!"
He shrugged.
"Prove it."
I couldn't.
So I did what any reasonable person would do.
I walked over to his poster and drew a tiny mustache on his picture too.
Noah covered his face.
"Tim..."
"What?"
"You just started a war."

ROUND ONE
By break time, Brad had put up another poster.
It said:
BRAD WILL MAKE SCHOOL BETTER!
I put up one next to it.
TIM WILL MAKE SCHOOL LESS BORING!
Brad added:
BRAD IS STRONGER!
I added:
TIM IS SMARTER!
Brad:
BRAD IS COOLER!
Me:
TIM HAS BETTER HAIR!
Brad looked at my hair.
Then he looked at his.
"Okay, that's debatable."
I knew I had him.

ROUND TWO
At lunch, things got worse.
Brad announced that he was going to prove he was the best candidate by doing a push-up competition.
Nobody had asked him to do this.
But suddenly everyone was watching.
Brad dropped to the ground.
"ONE!"
"Two!"
"Three!"
He kept going.
I looked at Noah.
"Do I have to do this?"
"No."
"Good."
Brad reached thirty push-ups.
Then he looked at me.
"Your turn."
Everyone started chanting:
"TIM! TIM! TIM!"
This was a problem.
I had done approximately four push-ups in my entire life.
But I couldn't back down.
So I got on the ground.
"One."
My arms were already shaking.
"Two."
I started regretting everything.
"Three."
Then I collapsed.
Everyone went silent.
I slowly stood up.
"That was just a warm-up."
Noah started laughing so hard he nearly fell over.
Brad laughed too.
But then something unexpected happened.
A kid shouted:
"TIM STILL RULES!"
Everyone started cheering.
I smiled.
Maybe losing wasn't so bad.

ROUND THREE
After lunch, I discovered that Brad had started giving out free chocolate bars.
I looked at Noah.
"That's cheating."
"It's not technically cheating."
"It's bribery."
"Still not technically cheating."
I reached into my backpack.
I had one packet of chips.
I held it up.
"FREE CHIPS FOR EVERYONE!"
Suddenly, people surrounded me.
"TIM!"
"TIM, GIVE ME ONE!"
"TIM IS THE BEST!"
Brad stared at me.
"You can't beat me with chips."
I smiled.
"Watch me."
Within thirty seconds, the chips were gone.
I had completely forgotten that I actually wanted to eat them.
But at least my campaign was working.
Sort of.

THE FINAL BATTLE
After school, Brad walked up to me.
"Tomorrow."
"What?"
"We settle this."
"Settle what?"
"The election."
I sighed.
"Okay. How?"
Brad pointed toward the basketball court.
"One final challenge."
"What kind?"
He smiled.
"You'll find out."
That didn't sound good.
Noah walked over.
"What did he say?"
I looked at him.
"Apparently we're having a final challenge tomorrow."
Noah thought for a second.
Then he said:
"Should I start preparing your funeral?"
"Thanks."
"No problem."
I walked home wondering what Brad could possibly have planned.
And then I realized something.
Tomorrow was the school talent show.
Oh no.
Brad was going to make me compete in the talent show.
The only problem was...
I DON'T HAVE A TALENT.

## Chapter 3: My Totally Amazing Talent

Wednesday
I woke up this morning with one thought in my head:
I HAVE NO TALENT.
I tried thinking of something I could do for the talent show.
I can play video games.
But apparently sitting on a couch and pressing buttons isn't considered a talent.
I can eat a lot of chips.
But Mom said that's "not something to be proud of."
I can sleep for twelve hours straight.
That one was actually impressive, but I didn't think the teachers would let me perform it.
Then I remembered something.
Something I had been practicing for years without even realizing it.
I can make extremely weird noises with my mouth.
Not normal noises.
I can make a noise that sounds like a duck choking.
I can make a noise that sounds like a car starting.
And I can make this weird squeaking sound that I discovered when I was six.
I don't know why I know how to do these things.
But I do.
I immediately called Noah.
"I've got my talent!"
"What is it?"
"I can make weird noises."
There was a long silence.
Then Noah said:
"Please don't."
"Why?"
"Because I have to sit in the audience."
"That's the point."

AT SCHOOL
When I arrived at school, Brad was already waiting for me.
"So," he said, "what's your talent?"
I wasn't going to tell him.
Then I made the mistake of bragging.
"Something amazing."
Brad laughed.
"I'll believe it when I see it."
Noah walked past us.
"Trust me, Brad. You don't want to see it."
Brad looked confused.
I smiled.
"Exactly."

THE TALENT SHOW
After lunch, everyone gathered in the hall.
There were about fifty students sitting in the audience.
The teacher walked onto the stage.
"Welcome to the annual school talent show!"
Everyone clapped.
First, a girl played the piano.
Then someone did magic tricks.
Then Brad went on stage.
Of course.
His talent was apparently doing basketball tricks.
He bounced the ball behind his back.
Then between his legs.
Then he threw it in the air and caught it.
Everyone cheered.
Brad looked at me from the stage.
He gave me a thumbs-up.
I wanted to disappear.
Then the teacher said:
"Next up... Tim!"
My stomach dropped.
I slowly walked onto the stage.
The entire hall was staring at me.
I looked at Noah.
He was already laughing.
I took a deep breath.
"Okay."
I walked up to the microphone.
"My talent is..."
I stopped.
"...making weird noises."
Nobody said anything.
Then someone in the back shouted:
"WHAT?"
I closed my eyes.
This was a terrible idea.
But I had already started.
I took a deep breath.
And made the duck noise.
"QUAAAAAAK-HRRRRRKK!"
The entire hall went silent.
Then—
Someone laughed.
Then another person laughed.
Then the whole room exploded.
I started laughing too.
So I did the car noise.
"BRRRRRRMMMMM-BRRRMMMM!"
Then the squeak.
"EEEEEEEEEEK!"
People were practically falling out of their chairs.
Even the teacher was laughing.
I finished with my best noise.
The sound of a dying printer.
"BEEP... BEEP... KRRRRR... EEEEE... BZZZZZZ!"
The audience went crazy.
I stood there completely confused.
I had expected everyone to make fun of me.
Instead, people were cheering.
I looked over at Brad.
He wasn't laughing.
He looked worried.
Very worried.

AFTER THE SHOW
When I got off the stage, Noah ran over.
"TIM!"
"What?"
"That was actually amazing!"
"I know."
"You looked like an idiot."
"I know."
"But everyone loved it."
I smiled.
Maybe having a weird talent wasn't so bad.
Then Brad walked over.
"Congratulations."
I stared at him.
"Thanks?"
He looked serious.
"But you haven't won yet."
"What do you mean?"
He pointed at the stage.
"The judges are announcing the winner tomorrow."
I suddenly remembered the election.
The talent show was supposed to be our final challenge.
If I won...
I might actually beat Brad.
Brad walked away.
Noah looked at me.
"You realize what's happening, right?"
"What?"
"You might actually become student council leader."
I smiled.
Then I heard a loud voice behind me.
"TIM!"
I turned around.
It was the principal.
He looked angry.
"Can I speak to you for a moment?"
My smile disappeared.
Uh-oh.
I was pretty sure principals don't usually ask to talk to you after you make a dying-printer noise in front of the entire school.

"Thanks."

Tomorrow.
The same day the election results would be announced.
And Brad was going to be there.

"We make your performance HUGE."

"Trust me."
I immediately knew that was a bad sign.

Noah brought a speaker.

By the end, we had created what Noah called:
THE TIM NOISE SHOW.
It included:

The duck noise

Then Noah had an idea.

This was going to be a disaster.

Tomorrow, Tim Rules.
